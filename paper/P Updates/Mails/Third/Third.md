Dear all,

We are pleased to announce the Generative AI and Experiments Conference, a one-day conference to be held at Columbia University on Friday, October 23, 2026.

Generative AI is reshaping how people seek, interpret, and share information, including in political settings where its use and misuse are growing. It is also transforming how we study these questions: large language models now serve as experimental treatments, conversational partners, measurement tools, and research agents. This conference brings together researchers developing new methods and applications, with panels and lightning talks throughout the day, concluding with a discussion of generative AI and the future of experimental design.

Confirmed speakers include Lisa Argyle (Purdue), Jennifer Jerit (Dartmouth), Diana Mutz (UPenn), Ethan Porter (GWU), Diana Jordan (Duke), Chris Barrie (NYU), Joshua Tucker (NYU), Jay Van Bavel (NYU), and Yamil Velez (Columbia). A tentative schedule is attached.

Date: Friday, October 23, 2026, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM

Location: Lindsay Rogers Room (707), IAB

Please pre-register to attend the conference using this link by Friday, October 16th. All registered guests will be provided with breakfast and lunch, and are invited to join the speakers for a reception after the conference.

This event is sponsored by the Institute for Social and Economic Research and Policy (ISERP) and the Department of Political Science at Columbia University.

We hope you’ll join us!
